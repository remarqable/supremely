# Code of Conduct

Supremely should be a place where people can disagree strongly about software without making participation unpleasant.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Assume good faith until there is evidence otherwise. Give technical arguments room to win on their merits.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate humiliation, spam, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Technical disagreement

Disagreement is normal and useful.

Maintainers may make final decisions when consensus is not possible. A rejected proposal is not a rejection of the person who proposed it.

## Enforcement

Serious or repeated violations may result in content removal or temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Security reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this process.
